About

I am a software engineer with more than five years of industry experience building reliable, large-scale backend and distributed systems. Currently I work on the payments team at MarginEdge (Arlington, VA; remote). I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), with the degree conferred by Bangladesh University of Professionals (BUP).

My undergraduate thesis, supervised by Dr. Md. Hasanul Kabir, built a deep convolutional neural network for retinal blood-vessel segmentation and used the resulting vessel maps for biometric identification. I now want to return to research full-time and study how machines can understand people in visual scenes: what they do, where they look, and what they are about to do. I want to pair that with the engineering discipline needed to make such models dependable in real-world use.
